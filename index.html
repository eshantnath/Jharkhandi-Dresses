Discover the Traditional Fashion of Jharkhand



Jharkhand is a land of rich tribal traditions, colourful festivals, folk music, dance and beautiful traditional clothing.

The traditional dresses of different communities represent their history, lifestyle, culture and identity.

Explore the ten traditional dress styles below.


Culture & Heritage of Jharkhand

Traditional Clothing

Traditional clothing is an important part of Jharkhand's cultural heritage. Different tribal communities have their own styles of clothing, ornaments and ways of dressing.

Festivals

Traditional clothes are especially visible during festivals, weddings, cultural programs, dances and community celebrations.

Tribal Communities

Jharkhand is home to several tribal communities, including Santhal, Munda, Oraon, Ho, Kharia, Birhor, Korwa, Birjia and Paharia communities.
